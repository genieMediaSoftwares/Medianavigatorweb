# Release launcher assets

Prepared on 23 October 2026 with the built-in image generation/editing tool after the owner selected the existing Media Navigator brand mark. These are cleaned raster adaptations, not pixel-identical copies of the original. Review on physical launch devices before store submission.

- Source reference: `web/public/brand/mark.png` (existing Media Navigator M and paper-plane mark).
- `launcher-icon.png`: 1254 × 1254, opaque RGB, white square, for the Expo app/iOS icon. Expo generates the native icon sizes.
- `adaptive-foreground.png`: 1254 × 1254, RGBA, padded foreground for Android adaptive masks, on the configured white background.
- The original assets and the in-app wordmark remain available. No Expo starter monochrome icon is configured.

## Prompt set

Launcher: “Preserve the existing blue folded M and paper-plane mark, silhouette, geometry, cyan/royal-blue/navy colours, proportions and folds. Clean stray edge pixels. Centre on an opaque white square with safe padding. No new logo, text, rounded container, badges or external shadows.”

Android foreground: “Preserve the prepared mark and its colours and folds. Remove the white background to alpha. Fit the complete mark inside the central 54 percent of a square transparent canvas, leaving at least 23 percent transparent padding per side. No new text, container, shadow or background.”
